App Builders PH embed pages:

- `certificat-appbuilders.webp` — `https://appbuildersph.com/embed/apps/certificat`
- `tidypaw-appbuilders.webp` — `https://appbuildersph.com/embed/apps/tidypaw-photo-cleaner`

They are displayed at their original colors over the monochrome live vote widgets.
